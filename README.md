## Problem Statement
*  Students track spending inconsistently, sometimes losing money or getting lost in budget management.
Similarly, generic planners focus on totals without insight given.* **We needed a tool that:**
- gives actionable feedback
- gives a *weekly digest* you can understand in 10 seconds
- keeps input simple
- preferrably works online
